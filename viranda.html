Viranda.
Commerce meets configuration.

A non-Shopify commerce project combining a Next.js and React storefront with Medusa.js, a Node.js backend and PostgreSQL. Its distinctive feature is a custom plant-and-pot configurator with compatibility rules, 2D/3D visualization and lighting effects.
