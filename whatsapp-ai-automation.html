01 / THE PROBLEM

Reduce manual work across the buying journey.

Cash-on-delivery merchants need to check whether customers intend to accept their orders; carts can be abandoned before checkout; and customers often need quick advice before choosing a product.

I built automation across those touchpoints, using WhatsApp for customer interaction and Shopify for operational visibility.

02 / IMPLEMENTED FEATURES

Three flows, one clearer customer journey.

01 / CASH ON DELIVERY

Order confirmation & cancellation

An order-confirmation message gives the customer a choice to confirm or cancel. That choice is reflected on the matching Shopify order through a tag, helping the merchant review cash-on-delivery orders.

02 / RECOVERY

Abandoned checkout follow-up

WhatsApp reminders reach customers who left the checkout unfinished, with a recovery link containing a welcome discount code intended to encourage completion.

03 / AI COMMERCE

Conversational product advice

Customers can ask the AI-powered assistant questions about products and receive relevant product suggestions during their shopping journey.
