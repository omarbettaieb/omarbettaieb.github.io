01 / THE PROBLEM

An ad platform's purchase count is not the same as real business results.

E-commerce teams often see purchase numbers in their advertising platform that differ from real Shopify orders. Cancellations, returns and invalid or suspicious orders can further distort the picture. Media spend alone does not reveal net profitability.

I built VegaProfit to reconcile these views and give teams one place to understand what happened, what it cost and which campaigns deserve attention.

03 / ANALYTICS & RECONCILIATION

From reported sales to validated revenue.

Three views make the business side inspectable: Profit tracks gross and net profitability across time, Meta Ads compares claimed purchases with attributable valid orders, and Real Revenue separates orders from cancellations, returns, fakes and delivery states.

UTM parameters support campaign-to-order attribution. The dashboard also exposes inconsistencies between ad-platform reporting and Shopify order evidence.

04 / ACTIONABLE AUTOMATION

Explainable recommendations, with the human in control.

A configurable rules engine evaluates campaign-level costs and verified orders against break-even thresholds. It suggests Kill, Pause, Scale, Wait or Watch, with contextual reasons rather than opaque scores.

The app advises: it does not autonomously edit ad campaigns. Health checks surface integration issues, data freshness, UTM coverage and operational configuration.
