Privacy & Analytics

This is a professional portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages. You can browse projects and download the CV without consenting to audience measurement.

Your choice matters

Google Analytics 4 is optional and is only loaded after you accept analytics. If analytics has not yet been configured, no Google Analytics tag is loaded.

What is measured after consent?

Google Analytics may receive page views and usage data, including general device and approximate location information. Custom portfolio events measure clicks on CV PDF links, role-focused profile pages, project case studies and contact/LinkedIn links. Event parameters describe the destination category, not a visitor's name, email address or phone number.

Cookies and tracking

Before your opt-in, this portfolio does not load the GA4 tag. Your choice is saved locally in your browser so you do not have to answer on every page. After opt-in, Google's analytics tag may set first-party analytics cookies and process usage information under Google's terms. Advertising storage and ad personalization are kept disabled by this integration.

Change your mind

Use the 'Analytics settings' control in the website footer at any time to accept or reject analytics. If you reject after previously accepting, the page reloads to stop the GA4 tag from running in the current page.

Third-party services and contact

GitHub Pages delivers the site and may process technical request logs independently of this analytics choice. External destinations (LinkedIn, Shopify stores and other websites) have their own privacy practices. Questions about this portfolio can be sent to
