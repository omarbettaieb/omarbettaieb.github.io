01 / MARKETING APPROACH

Strategy before design.

A wellness product needs more than a good-looking ad. The message has to fit a specific audience, speak in their language and connect to a clear product offer. The same principle extends into the storefront, conversion journey and performance reporting.

01 / AUDIENCE

Find the angle

Family care, beauty routines, seasonal relevance, gifting and simple product education.

02 / CREATIVE

Make it resonate

Product-specific concepts with Tunisian derja and French copy, clear offers and a consistent premium aesthetic.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Connect the data

Read campaign spend, attribution, CPA, ROAS and profitability together — not as disconnected dashboard metrics.
